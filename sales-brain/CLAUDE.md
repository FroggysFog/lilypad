# Sales Intelligence Knowledge Base - LilyPad ERP

## Froggys Fog (froggysfog.com)
- Target: Haunted Attractions, Escape Rooms, Family Entertainment Centers (FECs), Theaters, Touring Acts.
- Core SKUs:
  - Swamp Juice (Extreme Long Hang Time Fog - 4 Gal Case / 55 Gal Drum)
  - Bog Fog (Dense Whiteout Ground Fog - 4 Gal Case)
  - Neutronic / Pro Haze (Clean non-glycol theatrical haze for lighting & laser tag)
  - Titan Series Fog & Haze Generators (Titan 1500, Titan HT6)
- Seasonality: Early-bird purchasing peaks July–August. Urgent restocks occur in September–October.

## Training Smoke (trainingsmoke.com)
- Target: Municipal Fire Departments, Volunteer Fire Companies, Industrial Safety, Hazmat Teams.
- Core SKUs:
  - Training Smoke XD (Extreme Density - Non-toxic, zero-residue for burn towers)
  - Training Smoke Q (Quick Dissipating - Search & rescue in commercial buildings)
  - FireBase SG Series (SG-3100 rugged smoke generators)
- Operational Points: Water-based, zero oily residue, safe for SCBA sensors and turnout gear, PO & Net-30 support.
